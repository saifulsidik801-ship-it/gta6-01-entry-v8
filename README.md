**MARISOL BAY — take the city, district by district.** An original open-world
crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty
($1,666). A sun-soaked island city you explore on foot and by car: traffic and
pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted level with police who respond
(including an air unit with a spotlight), missions and a story, on-foot combat,
a HUD with a minimap, a full map + stats screen, a garage, a start screen and
save slots — all shown in screenshots of the game itself.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# or: serve game/ with any static server, then open game/index.html
```

No build step, no network request: Three.js is vendored under
`game/vendor/`, everything else is plain files under `game/`.

## How to play

- **W A S D** drive · **SHIFT** nitro · **1–4** weapons · **Z / X / C** crew
  abilities · **G** garage · **M** map + stats · **ESC** title screen
- On foot (night-market mission): **W A S D** move, **mouse click** shoot —
  take out the Kerono Boys, watch the tracer and the enemy markers.
- Drive through the gold checkpoint rings to advance the mission stage and
  take a district. Lose the heat to drop the wanted stars.

## What is in the game

- **Chase (day, Kumala Strand):** low chase camera behind the big orange
  Rukan Pelo GT, beach boulevard, palms, two police cruisers, dense traffic,
  helicopter with a spotlight cone, siren light pools on the road.
- **Night market shootout (Old Pasaru, 19:31):** bright warm market lights,
  protagonist aiming from behind, tracer fire, red enemy markers, one enemy
  down, a crowd in bright clothes.
- **Map + stats:** full-screen menu with a 66.1% progress ring, nine
  checklist items, six named districts, career stats and three save slots.
- **Title screen:** giant gradient MARISOL BAY over a dusk city skyline with
  beach, palms and umbrellas; CONTINUE / NEW GAME / LOAD GAME / SETTINGS and
  save slots.
- **Garage (Palm Court):** a real interior — lift pad, workbench, neon sign,
  tire stack — with upgrades for the Rukan Pelo GT.
